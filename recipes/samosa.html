Samosa Recipe

Delicious Homemade Samosas

These baked samosas have all the flavor and heartiness without frying.

Although this is an easier, healthier version, they taste just as delicious as fried samosas. Serve warm, plain, or with chutney.

Ingredients

For Samosa Dough

  • 2 cups (250 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup (60 ml) oil or melted ghee
  • 6 tablespoons (90 ml) water
  • 3/4 teaspoon carom seeds
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt

For Potatol Filling

  • 4 medium (500 grams) potatoes
  • 1/2 cup green peas (boiled or frozen)
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • 1 tablespoon ginger or minced or paste
  • 1 to 2 green chillies
  • 1 pinch hing
  • 4 tablespoos coriander leavers
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

spices

  • 3/4 teaspon cumin seeds
  • 3/4 to 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 3/4 teaspoon red chlli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspon fennel powder

Steps

  • Prepare the Dough: In a bowl, combine all-purpose flour, carom seeds, and salt. Add oil or melted ghee and rub it into the flour until crumbly. Gradually add water and knead to form a firm, smooth dough. Cover and let it rest for at least 30 minutes.
  • Boil Potatoes: Boil the potatoes until tender, then peel and mash them coarsely.
  • Prepare the Filling: Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter. Add hing, ginger, and green chilies; sauté briefly. Stir in red chili powder, cumin powder, and fennel powder. Add the mashed potatoes, green peas, and salt; mix well. Cook for a few minutes until heated through. Remove from heat, stir in garam masala, lemon juice, and coriander leaves. Let the filling cool completely.
  • Shape the Samosas: Divide the dough into equal portions. Roll each portion into an oval or round shape, then cut it in half. Take one half, moisten the straight edge with water, and form a cone. Fill the cone with the potato filling. Seal the edges, ensuring a tight seal, to form the samosa shape.
  • Fry the Samosas: Heat oil for deep frying in a deep pan over medium-low heat. Carefully place a few samosas in the hot oil (do not overcrowd). Fry, turning occasionally, until they are golden brown and crispy on all sides.
  • Serve: Remove fried samosas and drain excess oil on paper towels. Serve hot with chutney.
