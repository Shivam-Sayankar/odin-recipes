Pani Puri Recipe

Tangy, Spicy Pani Puri!

Pani Puri, also known as Golgappa, is a beloved Indian street food. It features crispy, hollow puris filled with a spiced potato and chickpea mixture, then topped with tangy mint-tamarind water and often sweet tamarind chutney. Each bite is a burst of spicy, tangy, and sweet flavors!

Ingredients

For Puri (Crispy Shells):

  • 2 cups fine semolina (suji/rava)
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup warm water (approx., adjust as needed)
  • Oil for deep frying

For Spicy Mint & Tamarind Water (Teekha Pani):

  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves
  • 1-2 green chilies (adjust to spice preference)
  • 1 inch ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind pulp (or 2 tablespoons lemon juice)
  • 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black salt (kala namak)
  • 1/2 teaspoon chaat masala
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper powder
  • 2-3 cups chilled water
  • Salt to taste
  • Boondi (optional, for garnish)

For Sweet Tamarind Chutney (Meetha Pani or Imli Chutney - Optional but Recommended):

  • 1/4 cup seedless tamarind
  • 1/4 cup jaggery (gud) or brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup hot water
  • 1/4 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • A pinch of salt

For Potato & Chickpea Filling:

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled, peeled, and mashed coarsely
  • 1/2 cup boiled green peas or black chickpeas (kala chana)
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped onion (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh coriander leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon chaat masala
  • 1/4 teaspoon red chili powder (optional)
  • Black salt or regular salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Puri Dough: Combine semolina, all-purpose flour, baking soda (if using), and salt. Rub in oil/ghee, then gradually add warm water to knead a stiff dough. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Make the Teekha Pani: Blend mint leaves, coriander leaves, green chilies, ginger, tamarind pulp, roasted cumin powder, black salt, chaat masala, and black pepper powder with some chilled water until smooth. Strain the mixture, add remaining chilled water and salt to taste. Refrigerate for 1-2 hours. Add boondi before serving.
  3. Prepare Sweet Tamarind Chutney (Optional): Soak tamarind in hot water. Mash and extract pulp. Cook pulp with jaggery, roasted cumin powder, and salt until slightly thickened. Cool.
  4. Prepare the Filling: Mix mashed potatoes, boiled green peas/chickpeas, chopped onion (if using), coriander leaves, roasted cumin powder, chaat masala, red chili powder (if using), and salt.
  5. Roll and Fry Puris: Knead rested dough. Roll small portions into thin 2-3 inch discs. Heat oil for deep frying over medium-hot heat. Fry puris, gently pressing to help them puff, until golden brown and crispy. Drain on paper towels and cool completely.
  6. Assemble and Serve: Make a small hole in each puri. Fill with potato and chickpea mixture, then add sweet tamarind chutney (if using), and finally, generously fill with chilled teekha pani. Serve immediately.
